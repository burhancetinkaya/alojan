# M3/M4 real carrier validation — 2026-10-24

## Scope and environment

Owner-requested real inbound Netgsm test of M3-01/02 and M4-01/02/03;
US-01/02/04/05/06/07, requirements sections 6/7/9/11 and ADR 0007.
The existing live listener was used. The rejected dialogue-quality candidate
was not deployed. ASR (15173), EMA (15072) and agent (15073) health checks
returned SERVING. The running gateway arguments specify a 3000 ms answer delay
and enabled barge-in. Configuration alone is not handset acceptance evidence.
No service was restarted, source deployed, recording enabled or notification sent.

The owner was asked to introduce a short pause, leave a message with a delivery
day, interrupt the assistant with a corrected day, and explicitly finish. One
new real call was correlated with a private log cursor and database start time.
The stored active-call interval was 32.97 seconds; this excludes ringing and
parts of the opening and is not the complete handset call duration. Raw caller
identifiers and transcripts remain private and are not included here.

## Timing and limits

Five generations emitted response audio. Endpoint-decision to first audio ranged
from 899 to 2460 ms; ASR finalization ranged from 103 to 116 ms. The logged
estimated VAD-end-to-audio values ranged from 1872 to 3468 ms. These are different
measurement boundaries and a five-response sample, not P95/SLA acceptance.
One other generation was cancelled before emitting response audio.

Last-sent-packet timestamps were at or before the interruption decision, but this
does not measure caller speech onset or establish the <250 ms barge-in target.
The owner was asked whether speech audibly stopped and whether the final farewell
was followed by roughly three seconds before disconnection. The owner confirmed
disconnection and described the experience as very good and not disturbing;
no separate numeric delay or interruption-onset measurement was supplied.
The approximately one-second introduction pause is not sufficient on
its own to establish incomplete-sentence or false-interruption acceptance.

## Decision

The owner accepted the tested phone experience and confirmed that the call
closed. The owner deprioritized summary improvement, preferring to read the
stored conversation when needed. Retain the readable ASR transcript and final
message note as the primary review records; this does not make ASR output a
verbatim recording or remove the observed recognition concerns above.

Summary improvement is deferred, not a failed summary relabeled correct. The
observed farewell-priority deviation remains documented, although the owner did
not find this call uncomfortable. M3 gains real-call interruption/accounting
evidence; quantitative onset timing and broad M3/M4 acceptance are not established
by one satisfactory call. Preserve the current live version; do not promote the
previously rejected prompt candidate on this evidence. No summary service,
recording policy or product requirement was removed.
